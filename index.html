Settings

ES comes first. If the ES link fails or is blank, the app falls back to SPY, which only covers stock-market hours. Both are stored only on this phone. The app refreshes at most every 5 minutes.

ES prices come from Yahoo Finance's free feed, typically about 10 minutes delayed. The 4-hour window runs on the clock, round the clock, and stops at Friday's 5 PM ET close. These are statistics, not advice: a 55% setup still loses 45% of the time.
